Research Interests

My research interests lie at the intersection of multimodal perception and natural language understanding. I am particularly excited about:

Multimodal humor understanding. Studying how vision-language models can comprehend complex, incongruous relationships (like cartoons and caption contests).

Multimodal reasoning. Improving the logical and conceptual capacity of large language models when integrated with vision and other modalities.
